Why Great S Pen Notes Still Leave You Forgetting Half the Lecture

Samsung Notes is easily the best free handwriting app pre-installed on a Galaxy Tab, but it is a terrible place to keep material you actually need to memorize for an exam. I realized this back in late 2025 after watching students fill dozens of colorful S Pen folders during the semester, only to blank out on test day because passive note-taking never forces your brain to retrieve anything.

If you just want to mark up a professor's PDF slides with a stylus or record audio synced to handwritten ink, Samsung Notes is fantastic and you should keep it on your tablet. The problem starts the moment class ends and you need to turn those raw pages into long-term memory, or open your study notebooks on a custom Windows desktop or Linux ThinkPad that is not a Galaxy Book.
