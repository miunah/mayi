How to Pass Residência Médica While Surviving Internato

May 15, 2026 · 3 min read

Passing Residência Médica on ENARE, USP, UNICAMP, or UNIFESP comes down to one uncomfortable rule, doing thousands of past exam questions and turning your mistakes into short daily reviews while surviving internato shifts.

In early 2026, while talking with medical interns testing Mayinab between hospital rounds, I kept hearing the same frustration. Watching marathon prep-course lectures at 2x speed felt productive on Sunday night, then evaporated completely by Wednesday's shift.

Stop Worshipping 2x Lectures and Giant Premade Decks

Prep courses love selling endless lecture calendars, and shared decks with 15,000 cards look tempting at first glance. Both traps bury you under review fatigue by July.

The five major areas, Internal Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, OB-GYN, and Preventive Medicine, recycle the same clinical scores, cutoffs, and first-line treatments year after year. Think Light criteria, Jones criteria, CURB-65, ATLS trauma steps, vaccination schedules, and SUS legislation. You will remember those far better when every flashcard comes from a real question you actually got wrong.

Match Your Study to Your Target Hospitals

Starting in the 5th year with 15 to 20 mistake cards a day beats cramming everything in the 6th year. Align your question blocks with your current hospital rotation whenever possible, since seeing a real patient with pleural effusion or preeclampsia makes the exam guideline stick immediately.

By the final four months, run full timed simulations from your exact target exams, whether that is ENARE, USP-SP, UNICAMP, or Santa Casa. If your target institution has a practical OSCE phase or curriculum evaluation, drill station checklists early instead of hoping you can improvise after Phase 1.

Reviewing Between Shifts Without Signal

Hospital elevators and wards are notorious dead zones for mobile data, which is why we built Mayinab as an offline-first SQLite app. You open it during a ten-minute break, knock out 25 due cards, and close it without waiting for a cloud spinner.

Mayinab uses FSRS-5 spaced repetition so your review load adapts to how well you actually remember each protocol instead of spamming you with rigid SM-2 intervals. If you already rely on Anki CSV or TSV decks, you can import them and migrate legacy SM-2 scheduling automatically.

You can attach clinical images like ECG strips or X-rays, write dosing formulas in LaTeX, or sketch quick algorithms in Scribble. The app is 100% free and non-profit, with zero ads and no account required. Free sync works across Android, Windows, and Linux via LAN P2P, a monitored folder, or your own Google Drive appDataFolder, while iOS and macOS remain on the open waitlist. Optional BYOK AI or local Ollama is there if you want help trimming long guideline notes into quick Q&A cards.

You do not need an expensive subscription to remember clinical guidelines. Keep your question volume high, keep your deck lean, and protect those 30 minutes of daily review even on exhausting weeks.

Como Passar na Residência Médica Sobrevivendo ao Internato

15 de maio de 2026 · 3 min de leitura

Passar na Residência Médica pelo ENARE, USP, UNICAMP ou UNIFESP se resume a uma regra desconfortável, resolver milhares de questões comentadas e transformar seus erros em revisões curtas todo dia enquanto você sobrevive aos plantões do internato.

No começo de 2026, conversando com internos que testam o Mayinab entre um rodízio e outro, eu ouvi a mesma reclamação várias vezes. Assistir a maratonas de aula gravada em 2x no domingo à noite dava sensação de dever cumprido, mas sumia da memória antes do plantão de quarta.

Pare de cultuar aula em 2x e baralho gigante pronto

Cursinho caro adora vender cronograma interminável de vídeo, e baralho compartilhado com 15 mil cards parece tentador no primeiro mês. Os dois caminhos costumam levar ao mesmo burnout de revisão lá por julho.

As cinco grandes áreas, Clínica Médica, Cirurgia, Pediatria, Ginecologia-Obstetrícia e Preventiva, reciclam os mesmos critérios, pontos de corte e condutas de primeira linha todo ano. Pense em critérios de Light, Jones, CURB-65, protocolos do ATLS, calendário vacinal e legislação do SUS. Você grava tudo isso muito melhor quando cada flashcard nasce de uma questão real que você errou.

Alinhe o estudo às bancas que você vai prestar

Começar no 5º ano criando 15 a 20 cards de erro por dia evita o desespero do 6º ano. Sempre que der, case os blocos de questões com o rodízio atual do hospital, porque atender um caso real de derrame pleural ou pré-eclâmpsia faz o protocolo da prova grudar na hora.

Nos últimos quatro meses, rode simulados completos no tempo real das suas bancas alvo, seja ENARE, USP-SP, UNICAMP ou Santa Casa. Se o hospital tiver 2ª fase prática no formato OSCE ou análise curricular, treine checklists de estação cedo em vez de tentar improvisar depois da 1ª fase.

Revisão no intervalo do plantão sem depender de sinal
