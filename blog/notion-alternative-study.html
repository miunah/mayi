Notion is a great workspace for company wikis, but it is a terrible tool for passing tough exams because pretty pages do not help you remember anything under pressure. Back in early 2025, I wasted two full weekends building color-coded study dashboards in Notion, only to blank on half the material during my midterms.

I call this the note-and-forget trap. Notion makes storing paragraphs feel productive, so you keep tweaking icons and database columns instead of testing your memory.

Why Pretty Dashboards Fail During Exam Week

When you rely on Notion for class notes, you eventually face a painful choice. Either you re-read your toggles passively the night before the test, or you spend hours copying text over to Anki just to get real spaced repetition.

Wait until your campus Wi-Fi drops in a lecture hall and Notion's cloud-first architecture really stings. You stare at a loading spinner while the professor moves on to the next slide.

How We Designed Mayinab Around Actual Retention

When we started building Mayinab, we threw out databases, cover banners, and complex page trees. You open a subject, pick a topic, and type into a fast chat-style timeline that saves directly to a local SQLite database on your machine.

Right after typing a note with Markdown or LaTeX formulas, you type /flashcard and write your question in the same flow. The card stays attached to the exact context where you learned it, right next to audio recordings, checklists, PDFs, or quick sketches made with the Scribble drawing pad.

Every card is scheduled by FSRS-5, which tracks how your memory actually decays instead of using rigid thirty-year-old multipliers. If you already have Anki decks, you can import your CSV or TSV files and Mayinab migrates your old SM-2 history into FSRS-5 automatically.

When You Should Still Keep Notion Around

I still use Notion when I need to coordinate a group project, share a public syllabus, or track team tasks on a Kanban board. Mayinab is not built for multiplayer workspace management, and we have no interest in turning it into a corporate tool.

If your goal is studying alone and keeping full ownership of your data, Mayinab gives you everything without charging a cent. It is 100% free and non-profit, has zero ads, requires no account, and lets you sync across devices using LAN P2P, a monitored folder, or your personal Google Drive appDataFolder.

You can even plug in your own API key from OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, or run a free local Ollama model to generate review cards straight from your notes. Download Mayinab today on Android, Windows, or Linux, or join the waitlist if you are on iOS or macOS.
