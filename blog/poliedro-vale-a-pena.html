Why Poliedro Remains a Powerhouse (and a Trap for Passive Students)

Yes, Poliedro is worth the investment in 2026 if you are aiming for ultra-competitive programs like Medicine at USP, UNICAMP, or ITA, provided you already have the study stamina to survive its massive workload without drowning in unread textbooks. Talking daily with pre-med students using Mayinab in early 2026, I saw the exact same pattern play out every month.

Students who treat Poliedro's books and P+ platform like a question bank make huge score jumps by August. Students who try to watch every single lecture and copy colorful summaries fall three weeks behind by April and burn out.

What actually justifies Poliedro's steep price tag is the quality of its printed and digital textbooks and the difficulty calibration of its weekly mock exams. The math, physics, and chemistry lists push way deeper than standard ENEM prep platforms, which is mandatory when you face FUVEST second-phase essays.

Who Should Skip Poliedro in 2026

I do not recommend Poliedro if you are starting from zero foundation in high school math and science or if you only have two hours a day to study after work. The class schedule assumes you can spend the morning in lectures and the entire afternoon grinding through exercises.

If your budget is tight or you need a gentle step-by-step buildup, independent online platforms like Ferretto or Aprova Total give you much better cost efficiency without the crushing weekly pressure.
