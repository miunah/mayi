Mayinab beats Brainscape for almost any student who writes their own study material, because paying $9.99 a month just to get offline flashcards and unlimited decks makes zero sense in 2026. Brainscape has one real advantage, its library of pre-made certified decks for exams like the WSET wine certifications or specific US bar topics. If you just want to buy someone else's cards and tap buttons on your phone, their mobile app feels clean and gets the job done.

FSRS-5 vs Confidence-Based Repetition

When I reviewed 400 cards in Brainscape during January 2026, I noticed its Confidence-Based Repetition (CBR) doesn't actually schedule cards for a specific calendar date based on memory decay. You rate a card from 1 to 5, and Brainscape just cycles the low-confidence cards more often inside your current session loop.

That works fine for weekend cramming, yet it falls apart over a six-month exam prep cycle because old "5" cards keep popping up when you don't need them. Here at Mayinab, we use FSRS-5, which calculates your exact forgetting curve and pushes mastered cards weeks or months ahead. If you already have old decks, Mayinab imports Anki CSV or TSV files and migrates legacy SM-2 schedules automatically.

Why Flashcards Need Notes Next to Them

My biggest frustration with Brainscape is that cards float in a vacuum without context. When I fail a card three weeks later, I have to open another app just to read my original lecture summary.

We built Mayinab as a chat-style timeline where your text notes, flashcards, checklists, audio recordings, files, and Scribble drawings live in the same scroll. You also get native Markdown and LaTeX for math or code, plus an optional BYOK AI assistant where you plug in your own OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, or free local Ollama key to generate cards from your notes.

Offline Storage and the $120/Year Question

Locking offline mode behind a $9.99 monthly subscription is hostile to students who commute on the subway or study in campus basements with bad Wi-Fi. Mayinab stores everything in a local SQLite database on your device with zero ads and no account required.

To keep your phone and PC in sync for free, you can pair devices over LAN P2P via QR Code or Tailscale, point to a monitored folder with Syncthing, Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox, or connect your own Google Drive appDataFolder.

Stick with Brainscape only if you are paying specifically for one of their licensed marketplace courses or need an iPhone app right this second. Otherwise, grab Mayinab on Android, Windows, or Linux today (or join our iOS and macOS waitlist) and keep that $120 a year in your pocket.

FSRS-5 vs Repetição Baseada em Confiança

Quando revisei cerca de 400 cartões no Brainscape em janeiro de 2026, percebi que o sistema CBR deles não agenda revisões para datas reais calculando a curva de esquecimento. Você dá uma nota de 1 a 5 e o app apenas repete as notas baixas com mais frequência dentro de uma fila contínua.

Isso funciona para decorar matéria na véspera da prova, mas cansa rápido em estudos de seis meses para concurso ou vestibular porque cartões nota 5 continuam voltando sem necessidade. Aqui no Mayinab a gente usa o FSRS-5, que calcula sua retenção real e joga o que você já domina para semanas ou meses lá na frente. Se você já tem material antigo, o app importa arquivos CSV ou TSV do Anki e migra o histórico SM-2 automaticamente.
