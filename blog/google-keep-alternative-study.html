Great for groceries, bad for studying

Keep is simple, free and already on most Android phones, so I get why people start there. In early 2025 I tried to run a whole semester of notes in it, and it fell apart after about three weeks.

Long notes turn into a mess after a few paragraphs. Labels and colors are the only organization, so nothing is easy to find. There are no flashcards and no spaced repetition, which means you can write all day and still not remember any of it.

And on a computer it is web only, with no real desktop app.

What changed when I moved my notes

The first thing I noticed was structure. Everything sits under a subject and a topic, with a sidebar, so I stopped scrolling through colored squares.

Then the cards. I write a note in class, type /flashcard, and the review gets scheduled for me by FSRS-5. Markdown and LaTeX work, so my math and code notes finally look right.

On Windows and Linux there is a desktop app with split-view and a command palette on Ctrl+K. It also runs on Android. iOS and macOS are not out yet, but the waitlist is open.

Privacy was a bonus I did not expect to care about. Keep needs a Google account and lives on Google's servers. Mayinab is offline-first, with no account and no ads. To sync devices you can use your local network with a QR code or Tailscale, a watched folder, or your own Google Drive.

Who should stay on Keep

If you only need quick reminders, a grocery list or tight Google Workspace integration, stay on Keep. It does that job well and has no learning curve.

If you are studying for an exam, a language or a certification and want to actually remember it, you need something built for that. Keep is scratch paper. Mayinab is the notebook and the flashcards together.

Ótimo para lista de compras, ruim para estudar

O Keep é simples, grátis e já vem na maioria dos Android, então entendo por que todo mundo começa nele. No começo de 2025 tentei tocar um semestre inteiro de anotações lá, e a coisa desandou em umas três semanas.

O que mudou quando troquei de app
