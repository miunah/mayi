Best App for Self Notes and Self-Study: Build Your Personal Knowledge Base

November 20, 2025 · 3 min read

Mayinab is the best app for self notes and self-study in 2026 because it puts fast chat-style note capture and FSRS-5 spaced repetition flashcards in the exact same timeline. Back in late 2025, I realized I had over four hundred personal study notes sitting in Obsidian and Google Keep that I had not opened once since writing them.

Most note-to-self apps fail because they act like digital attics. You dump a book summary, a code snippet, or a language rule into a folder and immediately forget it exists.

Why Traditional Note Apps Fail Self-Learners

In early 2026, after three months of testing Notion, Obsidian, and Apple Notes with students who study on their own, we saw the same pattern repeat. People spend hours tweaking tags and backlinks in Obsidian or formatting tables in Notion, yet retain almost nothing a week later.

Quick capture tools like Google Keep or messaging yourself on WhatsApp have the opposite flaw. Sending a message to yourself takes two seconds, which feels great in the moment, until your study insights get buried under grocery lists and memes with zero review schedule.

Anki fixes memory retention, except writing long explanations or rough study thoughts inside a pure flashcard app feels awful. You end up splitting your brain between two disconnected apps.

Chat-Style Capture Plus FSRS-5 Retention

We built Mayinab to feel as fast as texting yourself on Telegram while running a real memory engine underneath. You organize your self-study into Subjects and Topics, open a topic, type your thought, and press enter.

Right below that note, you can type /flashcard to turn a key concept into a review card without leaving the timeline. When you review that card later, your original notes, voice memos, PDFs, or sketches made with the Scribble drawing engine sit right next to it for context.

Instead of the outdated SM-2 algorithm from the 1980s, Mayinab runs on FSRS-5. When I migrated my personal programming and Japanese decks from legacy SM-2 to FSRS-5 in February 2026, my daily review count dropped by nearly a third while my recall stayed above 90 percent. If you already use Anki, Mayinab imports your CSV or TSV files and upgrades old SM-2 schedules automatically.

Offline SQLite, Free Sync, and Optional Local AI

Personal study notes belong on your hardware, not locked inside a paid subscription cloud. Mayinab is 100% free and non-profit, stores everything in an offline-first SQLite database with zero ads, and never asks you to create an account.

When I study on my Linux desktop and want to review on my Android phone on the couch, I sync for free over LAN P2P using a quick QR code or Tailscale. You can also sync through a monitored folder with Syncthing, Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox, or connect your own Google Drive appDataFolder.

If you want AI help to quiz yourself or turn rough notes into flashcards, Mayinab includes an optional BYOK AI assistant. You can plug in your own API key for OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Groq, or OpenRouter, or run a completely free local model through Ollama offline.

Mayinab is available today on Android, Windows, and Linux, and the waitlist for iOS and macOS is open on our site. If you are tired of hoarding dead notes you never read twice, download it and turn your next self-study session into memory that sticks.
