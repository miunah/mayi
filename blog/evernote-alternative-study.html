Why I dropped Evernote for studying

Yes, you can drop Evernote for studying. It stores notes well but never had flashcards or spaced repetition, which is the part that makes you remember anything. Mayinab is the app I built to fill that gap.

Back in early 2026, after a few months of fighting the free plan and the upsell prompts, I moved my own exam notes out of Evernote. I haven't gone back, but I'll be fair about what I lost.

What went wrong with Evernote

The free plan got squeezed down to one device and tight upload limits, and the paid plans got pricey after the Bending Spoons acquisition. The app also felt slower and busier to me after the rewrite.

If you only clip recipes, none of that matters. If you are a student who needs notes on the phone, the laptop and the library computer, it matters a lot.

Why Mayinab works better for studying

You write a note, type /flashcard right there, and the card lands in the same timeline. No exporting to Anki or Quizlet, no second app.

Reviews run on FSRS-5, which is newer than the old SM-2 most apps still use. It imports your Anki CSV or TSV, and it migrates old SM-2 data for you.

Notes support Markdown and LaTeX, so math and code look right. Everything is offline-first on SQLite, with no account and no ads, and the whole thing is free and non-profit.

Sync is free too. You can use your local network with a QR code or Tailscale, a monitored folder like Syncthing, Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, or your own Google Drive appDataFolder. There is also an optional AI assistant where you bring your own key, or run Ollama locally, plus a Scribble drawing engine.

When I would stay on Evernote

Keep Evernote if you rely on the web clipper, document scanning or email-to-notebook, or if you have years of clipped material you don't want to move. Mayinab doesn't do those things.

For exam prep I stopped missing the clipper after about a week, because what I really needed was flashcards.

Mayinab runs on Android, Windows and Linux today. The iOS and macOS waitlist is open, so if you live on an iPhone, wait a little.
