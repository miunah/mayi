Stop Using Two Apps to Study. Combine Notes and Flashcards in One.

January 25, 2026 · 3 min read

Splitting your study flow between Notion for notes and Anki for flashcards is the fastest way to kill your daily review habit. Back in late 2025, I realized I was spending more evenings copying and pasting bullet points between windows than actually testing my memory.

Almost every student I talk to hits this exact wall. You take great notes during a lecture, tell yourself you will turn them into cards on Sunday, and by the time Sunday arrives you are too exhausted to format fifty new prompts.

Why the Two-App Routine Breaks Down

Active recall works best when you write the question right while the concept is fresh in your head. The moment you have to alt-tab into a separate deck builder, pick a deck, and lose sight of your surrounding notes, friction wins.

I tested Quizlet and RemNote for a couple of months in early 2026 to see if they fixed this. Quizlet felt too shallow for dense notes and pushed paid subscriptions constantly, while RemNote buried simple flashcards under a steep outliner curve that slowed me down during fast classes.

How We Built Mayinab to Fix This

Here at Mayinab, we wanted note-taking and card creation to happen in the exact same input bar. Instead of rigid document pages, your subjects and topics look like a clean chat timeline where you just type and hit send.

When you write a paragraph about pharmacology or rust ownership and want to test yourself on it tomorrow, you just type /flashcard right below that note. The card sits inline with the explanation that sparked it, alongside checklists, audio clips, PDFs, or quick diagrams drawn on the built-in Scribble pad.

On Windows and Linux, I love using the resizable split-view to read my timeline on the left while blasting through due reviews on the right. Every card runs natively on the FSRS-5 algorithm, which adapts to your memory much more accurately than legacy schedulers and requires fewer reviews for the same retention.

Keeping Your Existing Workflow Intact

You do not have to throw away years of work to switch. If you already have Anki decks, you can import your CSV or TSV files directly into Mayinab, and the app automatically migrates legacy SM-2 scheduling data into FSRS-5.

For group projects or shared class wikis, I still tell people to keep using Notion or Google Docs because Mayinab is built strictly for solo learning. Your database lives locally in offline-first SQLite with zero ads and no account required.

When you want your notes and cards on both your PC and your phone, you can sync for free via LAN P2P with a QR Code or Tailscale, a monitored folder like Syncthing or Dropbox, or your own Google Drive appDataFolder. We also included an optional BYOK AI assistant where you can plug in your own API key from Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek, or run a free local Ollama model to draft cards from your notes.

Mayinab is 100% free and non-profit, available today on Android, Windows, and Linux, with our iOS and macOS waitlist open right now. Stop wasting your study sessions on copy-paste chores and try keeping your context and your flashcards in one place.

Criar boas perguntas exige que o conceito ainda esteja fresco na cabeça. No instante em que você precisa trocar de janela, escolher baralho e perder de vista o parágrafo que acabou de escrever, o cansaço vence e a revisão fica para nunca.

O Quizlet é raso demais para quem precisa anotar teoria pesada e vive empurrando assinatura paga, enquanto o RemNote esconde funções simples atrás de uma estrutura complexa que me atrasava durante aulas rápidas.

Como Desenhamos o Mayinab para Resolver Isso

Aqui no Mayinab, a gente queria que anotar e criar flashcards acontecesse na mesma barra de digitação. Em vez de páginas engessadas, suas matérias e tópicos funcionam como uma timeline de chat onde você só digita e envia.
