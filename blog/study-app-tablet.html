That frustration is why we built Mayinab with real split-view panels and a resizable sidebar for wide screens. You can keep your summary open on the left while reviewing FSRS-5 flashcards on the right without ever switching windows.

Why Handwriting Apps Alone Fail Before Exam Day

Apps like Samsung Notes or Goodnotes feel great during a lecture when you want to scribble on a PDF. After three months of watching students prep for finals in late 2025, I noticed that purely handwritten notebooks rarely get reviewed again because there is no active recall built in.

My favorite tablet routine combines both worlds instead of forcing one over the other. You can check our Samsung Notes alternative guide to see how students capture raw class scribbles first and then turn key concepts into typed notes and inline flashcards inside Mayinab.

Your drawings, LaTeX math equations, Markdown notes, audio clips, and flashcards all sit together in the exact same topic.

Offline SQLite and Free Sync Across Devices

Most students buy Wi-Fi-only tablets without a cellular plan, which makes cloud-only apps useless on a bus or in a campus basement. Mayinab is 100% offline-first and stores everything in a local SQLite database on your tablet, with zero ads, no account required, and no paywall.

When you get back on Wi-Fi and want to review on your laptop or phone, you do not need a paid cloud subscription. We built free multi-device sync via local LAN P2P using a QR Code or Tailscale, a monitored folder for Syncthing, Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox, or your own Google Drive appDataFolder.
