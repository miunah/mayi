Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know

There is no catch. Mayinab is currently 100% free and non-profit, with no plans to change that. There are no ads, no subscriptions, no locked features, and no paid cloud backend (we do not use Supabase or any commercial tracking servers).
You have three free, account-free or self-owned options: (1) LAN Sync P2P over Wi-Fi or Tailscale using a 4-digit PIN & QR Code; (2) Monitored Folder Auto-Sync via Syncthing, OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud, or Google Drive Desktop; (3) Google Drive Auto-Sync in your own private appDataFolder. All use Smart Merge with Tombstones so changes merge cleanly.
Yes. Everything runs locally on your device in SQLite with zero telemetry. When using the BYOK AI Assistant, requests go directly from your device to your chosen provider (or 100% locally via Ollama). Your API key is stored outside SQLite and is never included in backups or sync snapshots.
FSRS-5 (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler v5) is the state-of-the-art memory scheduling algorithm that adapts to your exact retention target (70%–97%) with fewer unnecessary reviews than SM-2. You can bulk-import Anki-compatible CSV/TSV decks (including YouTube URLs), and any legacy SM-2 cards migrate automatically to FSRS-5.
Mayinab charges nothing for the AI Assistant. It uses BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) so you can plug in OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Groq, DeepSeek, or OpenRouter — or run completely free and offline on desktop using Ollama. It includes 22 tools with explicit approval gating for sensitive actions.
Never. You can export individual subjects as portable .mayinab files (standard ZIP with JSON + media) or create complete local ZIP backups anytime. Since the app is 100% offline-first, it keeps working on your devices indefinitely.
