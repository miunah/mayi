Why I Stopped Trusting OneNote Before Exams

Mayinab is the best OneNote alternative for students because OneNote is a digital binder where class notes go to be forgotten, while Mayinab actually tests your memory with built-in FSRS-5 spaced repetition. I learned this the hard way back in late 2024 when my 5 GB OneDrive quota filled up right before finals and locked my notebooks into a sync conflict loop.

Even when OneNote synced properly, my study workflow was broken. I would spend hours organizing color-coded sections on an infinite canvas, only to forget 80 percent of the material a week later because passive rereading does not build long-term retention.

To actually remember anything for my certification exams, I had to copy my OneNote paragraphs into Anki every single night. That double work killed my momentum and eventually pushed us to build Mayinab.

How Studying Feels When Notes and Flashcards Share a Timeline

In Mayinab, you organize your life by Subject and Topic instead of losing text boxes off the right edge of an infinite page. You type your lecture notes in a fast chat-style timeline and drop a /flashcard command right under a tough concept.

After three months of testing our FSRS-5 scheduler with daily student users in early 2026, we saw how much time people save compared to legacy SM-2 apps. If you already have old Anki decks, you can import your CSV or TSV files and Mayinab migrates your SM-2 review history to FSRS-5 automatically.

STEM students also tell me how relieved they are to ditch OneNote's clunky equation editor. In Mayinab, you write standard Markdown and inline LaTeX like $E = mc^2$ at full typing speed, or open the Scribble drawing engine when you want to sketch a quick diagram by hand.

Local SQLite Privacy and Free Sync Without OneDrive

We built Mayinab as a 100% free, non-profit project with zero ads and no mandatory accounts. Your notes live locally on your device inside a fast SQLite database, so you never lose access on a train or in a campus basement without Wi-Fi.

When you want your notes on both your laptop and your phone, you do not need to pay Microsoft for extra cloud storage. You can sync across devices for free over local Wi-Fi using LAN P2P with a QR Code or Tailscale, point Mayinab to a monitored folder on Syncthing, Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox, or use your own Google Drive appDataFolder.

We also added an optional BYOK AI assistant that connects to OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, or a free local Ollama model on your own machine to turn rough notes into flashcards. None of your data touches a proprietary Mayinab server.
