Mayinab vs Quizlet: Which Flashcard App Is Better?

January 18, 2026 · 3 min read

Quizlet is still the fastest way to cram for a Friday vocabulary quiz using someone else's public deck, but it fails badly when you need to remember thousands of concepts six months from now. Back in late 2025, I watched students hit Quizlet's paywall after just a few practice rounds and lose access to their own cards on the subway.

That frustration is one of the main reasons we built Mayinab as a 100% free, non-profit, offline-first study tool. If you are preparing for long-term exams or certifications, you need a real memory algorithm and notes that live right next to your flashcards.

Why FSRS-5 Beats Cramming Modes

When I started testing review schedules in early 2026, the problem with Quizlet became obvious within two weeks. Quizlet treats studying like a short mini-game, which feels productive tonight but lets you forget half the material by next month.

Here at Mayinab, we use the FSRS-5 spaced repetition algorithm to calculate your exact memory stability after every single review. If you already use Anki, you can import your CSV or TSV files directly into Mayinab, and we migrate your legacy SM-2 review history to FSRS-5 automatically.

Paywalls, Ads, and Offline Freedom

Quizlet used to be generous, yet over the last couple of years almost every useful study mode moved behind the Quizlet Plus subscription. Free users get interrupted by banner ads and cannot even review their decks offline on a bus without paying.

Mayinab stores everything locally in a fast SQLite database on your device, with zero ads and no mandatory sign-up. You can sync across devices for free using LAN P2P via QR Code or Tailscale, a monitored folder with Syncthing, Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox, or your own Google Drive appDataFolder.

Writing Notes Where Your Cards Live

Studying only from isolated flashcards strips away context. In Mayinab, you write text notes, flashcards, checklists, audio clips, and hand-drawn diagrams with our Scribble engine inside one chat-style timeline, plus full Markdown and LaTeX support.

We also included an optional BYOK AI assistant where you plug in your own API key from OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, or run a free local Ollama model. You get instant card generation from your own notes without paying a monthly SaaS markup.

Stick with Quizlet if you just want to search millions of public sets five minutes before a casual class quiz, or if you rely on iOS and macOS right now while our Apple waitlist is still open. For everyone on Android, Windows, or Linux who builds their own study material and wants to remember it for years without paying a cent, download Mayinab and test the difference today.

Por Que o FSRS-5 Supera os Modos de Jogo

Quando eu comparei a retenção de longo prazo no começo de 2026, a limitação do Quizlet ficou evidente em duas semanas. O Quizlet trata a revisão como um joguinho rápido de combinar termos, o que dá uma sensação boa na hora, só que você esquece metade da matéria no mês seguinte.

Aqui no Mayinab, a gente usa o algoritmo FSRS-5 para calcular a estabilidade real da sua memória a cada resposta. Se você já tem decks no Anki, basta importar seus arquivos CSV ou TSV que o Mayinab migra todo o histórico antigo do SM-2 para o FSRS-5 automaticamente.

Paywall, Anúncios e Liberdade Offline

O Quizlet já foi bem aberto anos atrás, mas hoje quase todo modo útil de treino exige assinatura do Quizlet Plus. No plano gratuito, você leva propaganda na cara o tempo todo e não consegue revisar seus cards sem internet.

O Mayinab grava tudo localmente em um banco SQLite rápido no seu aparelho, sem anúncios e sem exigir cadastro. Para sincronizar entre dispositivos de graça, você escolhe entre rede local P2P via QR Code ou Tailscale, pasta monitorada com Syncthing, Drive, OneDrive ou Dropbox, ou direto no appDataFolder do seu próprio Google Drive.

Suas Notas no Mesmo Lugar dos Cards

Estudar só por cartões soltos tira todo o contexto da aula. No Mayinab, você mistura texto, flashcards, checklists, áudio, anexos e desenhos feitos à mão na engine Scribble dentro de uma timeline estilo chat, com suporte nativo a Markdown e LaTeX.

Continue no Quizlet se você só quer buscar listas prontas da comunidade cinco minutos antes de uma provinha rápida, ou se você depende exclusivamente de iOS e macOS enquanto nossa lista de espera da Apple está aberta.
