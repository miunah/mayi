October 21, 2025 · 3 min read

Yes, Anglo is still worth the tuition in 2026 if you are aiming at FUVEST, UNICAMP, UNESP, or a competitive medical seat, because its daily pacing forces discipline that cheap video platforms rarely replicate. If you only want a light, budget-friendly ENEM review, though, you will end up overpaying for pressure you do not need.

Over the first half of 2026, I talked to plenty of high-school seniors and repeat vestibular students in our Mayinab community who went through Anglo's schedule. Almost everyone praised the material and complained about the exact same bottleneck.

Why Anglo's structure still holds up

Anglo built its reputation on a blunt rule, class taught today gets studied today. The workbooks are carefully layered so you start with basic drills and climb quickly into the nasty open-ended questions São Paulo public universities love.

Their mock exams and essay feedback also sit a notch above generic online courses. You get realistic timing pressure all year instead of waking up in October realizing you write too slowly.

Where students usually burn out

That strict pace cuts both ways. Skip three afternoons because of school tests or exhaustion, and suddenly you are staring at a pile of unfinished exercise sheets that feels impossible to catch up on.

Price is the other catch, especially compared to online-only platforms like Ferretto or Aprova Total. And even when students finish every workbook on time in March, most of them tell me they barely remember early chemistry or history topics once November arrives.
