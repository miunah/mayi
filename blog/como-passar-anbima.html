How to Pass ANBIMA Certifications (CPA-10, CPA-20, CEA)

December 11, 2025 · 3 min read

Most candidates fail ANBIMA exams not because the math is brutal, but because they binge video lessons in two weeks and mix up tax rules, fund categories, and compliance details under time pressure. You need 70% to pass, and clearing that bar comfortably comes down to weekly mock tests paired with spaced repetition for every rule you miss.

In early 2026, several finance students using Mayinab shared how they prepped for CPA-10 and CPA-20 while working full-time bank shifts. Every single one who passed on the first try stopped rewatching long lectures and started turning wrong practice questions into small daily flashcard decks.

Exam Questions Passing Score Realistic Prep
CPA-10 50 (2h30) 70% (35 right) 30 to 60 days
CPA-20 60 (3h30) 70% (42 right) 60 to 90 days
CEA 70 (3h30) 70% (49 right) 90 to 120 days

If you already understand basic fixed income and funds, skipping CPA-10 and going straight to CPA-20 saves both exam fees and study months. Also double-check ANBIMA's official 2026 syllabus before booking your date, because ANBIMA has been rolling out its updated certification framework and fund rules under Resolution CVM 175.

Where people actually lose points

Investment Funds (now tested under Resolution CVM 175), Investment Products, Taxation, and Ethics/Compliance carry the bulk of the exam. People constantly trip over regressive Income Tax brackets (22.5% down to 15%), come-cotas timing in May and November, IOF sliding scales, FGC coverage limits, and COAF cash reporting rules.

On CPA-20 and CEA, derivatives (futures vs. forwards, swaps, options) and portfolio metrics like duration and Sharpe ratio separate safe passes from painful 66% failures. Do not treat Compliance as easy filler either, because ANBIMA loves practical client suitability scenarios.

A study routine that fits after work

Reading slides on the bus feels productive until the test asks a scenario question with two nearly identical options. Study one module in plain notes, run a timed question set immediately, and make a concise flashcard for every question you guessed or got wrong.

Antes de agendar, confira sempre o programa oficial vigente em 2026 no site da ANBIMA, já que a associação vem atualizando o formato das certificações e cobrando as regras da Resolução CVM 175 para fundos.

Onde a maioria perde pontos

Fundos de Investimento (já sob a Resolução CVM 175), Produtos de Investimento, Tributação e Compliance concentram o maior peso da nota. Muita gente escorrega na tabela regressiva de IR (de 22,5% até 15%), no come-cotas em maio e novembro, na tabela de IOF, nas regras do FGC e nos critérios de comunicação ao COAF.

Na CPA-20 e na CEA, derivativos (futuro, termo, swap e opções) e indicadores como duration e índice de Sharpe separam quem passa tranquilo de quem bate na trave com 66%. Não subestime Ética e Suitability achando que é ponto dado, porque a banca adora situações práticas de atendimento ao cliente.
